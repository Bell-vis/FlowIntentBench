# Carotid construction notes

Reviewed sources:

- VTK Examples, `CarotidFlow` (https://examples.vtk.org/site/Cxx/VisualizationAlgorithms/CarotidFlow/)
- VTK Book, Chapter 6, “Visualizing Blood Flow” (https://book.vtk.org/en/latest/VTKBook/06Chapter6.html)

The VTK Book directly ties `carotid.vtk` to human carotid blood-flow visualization and identifies the stored vector as blood velocity and the scalar as speed-proportional. The official example describes the streamtube and speed-isosurface pipeline. Those are separated into context and operationalization evidence. The source's discussion of resolution-related streamers leaving the artery is not emitted as a finding resource for the initial case.

Source records intentionally use the official documentation venues without guessing authors, dates, DOI, or report identifiers.
