# NASA_LOx_Post construction notes

Reviewed sources:

- VTK Examples, `LOx` (https://examples.vtk.org/site/Cxx/VisualizationAlgorithms/LOx/)
- VTK Book, Chapter 12, LOx Post case study (https://book.vtk.org/en/latest/VTKBook/12Chapter12.html)

The VTK Book directly identifies the NASA LOx Post files as a liquid-oxygen flow simulation across a flat plate with a cylindrical post, and places the case in a rocket-engine model. The official LOx example directly names `postxyz.bin` and `postq.bin` and describes computational-grid seeding for streamtubes. These become context and operationalization evidence respectively. The book's discussion of downstream flow structure is not emitted as a finding resource.
